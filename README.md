# Solevar‑Studio

Projects in **UI/UX design**, **AI automation**, **business analysis**, and **cybersecurity**.

Repositories include:
- Solutions for micro‑businesses and rapid deployment of digital products
- Tools for automating business processes and identifying technical solutions for specific needs
- Technical assessments of service compliance with the **AI Act**
- Security systems for online services, including products designed for children and parents
## Vision

Solevar‑Studio envisions a future where **AI systems** and **human creativity** collaborate seamlessly to build secure, ethical, and accessible digital products.  
Our focus is on developing **automation frameworks**, **design systems**, and **cyber‑resilience tools** that empower small teams and microbusinesses to operate with enterprise‑level efficiency.

We aim to:
- Integrate AI responsibly under the principles of the **EU AI Act**.  
- Create open‑source tools that accelerate product deployment and compliance.  
- Bridge the gap between creative design and technical engineering.  
- Support education and child‑focused digital development through safe AI solutions.
